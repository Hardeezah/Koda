NIGERIA AGRICULTURAL QUARANTINE SERVICE (NAQS)
EXPORT GUIDELINES FOR AGRICULTURAL COMMODITIES

1. INTRODUCTION
The Nigeria Agricultural Quarantine Service (NAQS) is the regulatory agency responsible for the issuance of Phytosanitary Certificates and Veterinary Health Certificates for the export of agricultural products (plants, animals, and their derivatives) from Nigeria, ensuring they meet international sanitary and phytosanitary (SPS) standards.

2. GENERAL EXPORT REQUIREMENTS
Exporters of agricultural commodities (e.g., cocoa, sesame seeds, cashew nuts, ginger, hibiscus, dried fish) must fulfill the following:
- Company Registration: The exporting entity must be registered with the Corporate Affairs Commission (CAC).
- NEPC Registration: Must possess a valid Exporter's Certificate issued by the Nigerian Export Promotion Council (NEPC).
- NXP Form: A completed Nigeria Export Proceeds (NXP) Form processed through an authorized dealer bank.

3. PHYTOSANITARY CERTIFICATION (PLANTS & PLANT PRODUCTS)
A Phytosanitary Certificate is mandatory for all plant-based exports.
- Procedure: The exporter must apply to NAQS for inspection of the consignment prior to loading.
- Inspection: NAQS officials will inspect the consignment to ensure it is free from pests, diseases, and prohibited contaminants.
- Fumigation: Depending on the destination country's requirements, the consignment may require fumigation by a NAQS-accredited fumigation company. A Certificate of Fumigation must be presented.
- Issuance: Upon satisfactory inspection, NAQS will issue the Phytosanitary Certificate.

4. ANIMAL AND ANIMAL PRODUCTS
For the export of animal products (e.g., hides, skins, dried fish, bone meal):
- A Veterinary Health Certificate issued by NAQS is mandatory.
- The exporter must provide evidence that the product originates from an area free of notifiable epizootic diseases.
- Additional health certificates from the National Agency for Food and Drug Administration and Control (NAFDAC) may be required for processed foods.

5. PROHIBITED EXPORTS
- Endangered species of flora and fauna (unless accompanied by a CITES permit).
- Unprocessed timber and rubber lump.
- Maize (under temporary export restriction).

6. FEES AND TIMELINES
- Processing Time: Inspections and certificate issuance typically take 48-72 hours upon formal application.
- Cost Estimate: Fees vary based on consignment volume and required treatments (e.g., fumigation), ranging from ₦10,000 to ₦50,000 per container.
